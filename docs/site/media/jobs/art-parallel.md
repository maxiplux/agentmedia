# Parallel section
Purpose: illustrate independent jobs running in parallel with safe recovery.
Subject: four side-by-side conveyor lanes each carrying a parcel; three parcels arrive at the end with green check badges while one lane is paused at a small amber caution marker, untouched by the others.
Composition: lanes running left to right, slight perspective.
Style: editorial flat illustration with a subtle paper grain, clean geometric shapes, soft shadows and generous negative space.
Palette: deep ink #172c33, cream paper #f8f8f2, forest teal #176653, burnt orange accent #ac491f, pale sage #eef2eb.
Do not render any text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks or readable interface words; suggest code or writing with simple bars and lines.
