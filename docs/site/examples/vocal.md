# Original short song
Mood: light, playful acoustic folk. One clear lead singer.
Duration: about twenty seconds; a short intro and a resolved ending.
Sing these original lyrics in this order:

One little seed, a patch of sun,
A quiet morning has begun.
Leaf by leaf, we watch it grow,
Small beginnings, nice and slow.

Do not add verses. Review the actual audio for omitted or changed lyrics.
