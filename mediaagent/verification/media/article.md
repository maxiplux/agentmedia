A morning walk is a chance to notice ordinary details. Mira pauses beside a tree, listens to the birds, and describes the path ahead. Kito checks the map and waits for the next instruction. They take turns speaking so every listener can follow the story. At the small bridge, they count twelve wooden boards and observe that three are shaded. They report what they can see without inventing facts. After crossing, they stop to review their route and compare notes. The lesson is simple: pay attention, describe details clearly, and give each other time to respond.
