# Same Mira and Kito, playing together in a forest

This is an identity-preserving edit of TWO specific existing characters, not a
redesign or a request for similar characters. Reference 1 is the exact Mira;
reference 2 is the exact Kito. Preserve each original as the SAME INDIVIDUAL.
The selected portraits control facial structure, proportions, and rendering style.
Do not use the rejected earlier forest image as a reference.

Mira: preserve the original face length and width, forehead and cheek geometry,
original eye outlines and spacing, nose size/shape, eyebrow shape, short black hair
silhouette and parting, and the original gentle toothy smile. Keep the same apparent
age, skin tone, original head-to-body ratio, olive jacket, orange scarf, cream
shirt, navy trousers and tan cross-body bag. Do not enlarge the eyes, open the mouth
widely, make the cheeks rounder, reshape the nose, age/de-age, or restyle the hair.

Kito: retain the original squat, compact toy proportions, broad rounded rectangular
head and visor, exact two oval amber eyes and small crescent smile, ivory shell,
teal shoulder caps/hands/feet, large orange circular chest button, and orange
antenna sphere. Keep its short solid legs and rounded body at the original ratios;
do not add articulated knees, lengthen the limbs/torso, shrink the head, or redesign
the face/mouth. Keep the original soft toy material and surface finish on both.

Change only the setting and a modest arm gesture: put the two original friends
in a bright forest clearing with ferns, tall green trees and a mossy fallen log.
They are playing a gentle game of catch with a small orange ball between them.
Mira is on the left, keeping her original nearly frontal head angle and original
smile, with one hand extended to pass the ball. Kito is on the right, retaining
its original nearly frontal face, compact stance and exact smile, with its hands
slightly raised to receive it. Avoid dramatic head rotations or exaggerated
expressions that would obscure whether they are the same characters.

Use soft diffuse warm light comparable to the original portraits. The forest
must match the characters' established toy-like style, not change their style to
match a new cinematic aesthetic. Show both complete characters with ample margins,
Mira's head and walking boots fully visible and Kito's antenna unobstructed.
Both should be the clear focus. No additional characters, no text, no logo.
