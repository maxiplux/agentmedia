# Identity acceptance checklist

Record each criterion as passed, needs_revision, or inconclusive, with expected,
observed, evidence, and location. Record findings through review record.

- Face geometry, eyes, hair silhouette and apparent age match the selected original.
- Head/body ratio, limb lengths, silhouette and distinguishing details match.
- Materials, palette and canonical accessories remain recognizable.
- Pose, expression and scene changes do not redefine stable identity.
- Cast members remain distinct and follow their own scene directions.
- Voice timbre, accent and pronunciation remain stable across three delivery clips.
- Requested dialogue, lyrics, numbers and units are preserved exactly.

Visual gate: three different solo scenes for each character, plus two paired
interaction scenes. Voice gate: three scripts and export/import reuse of a saved
binding. No unchecked or inconclusive criterion counts as human acceptance.
